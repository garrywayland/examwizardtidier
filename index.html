Superscripts: nothing converts automatically — any <sup>/<sub> markup from the source file is flattened to plain text on import. Anything that looks like it might need fixing (a letter directly followed by a digit, e.g. the "2" in "x2") is highlighted in yellow — click it to make superscript, Shift+click to make subscript, or Alt+click to leave it as plain text. You can also type ^2 (or ^{2n+1} / ^(2n+1) for multi-character powers) anywhere in the paper and it converts on its own shortly after you stop typing, or click any equation image to type its LaTeX by hand.
